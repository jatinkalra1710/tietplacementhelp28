FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No fee, no hidden charges, no paid tiers. Nobody involved is paid either.

Who can register?

Thapar students of batches 2028, 2029 and 2030, from any branch. Mentors are seniors and alumni of batch 2027 and earlier.

Is there any selection or test?

No. Register and you get access straight away. There is no test, no interview and no seat limit.

I’m in 2029 or 2030. Do I need a CGPA?

No. CGPA is optional for 2029 and 2030, and required for 2028 because it helps with referral eligibility.

Which email should I use?

The Google account you will sign in with. Approval is matched on that email.

How do mentors get approved?

An admin reviews every mentor registration (company, LinkedIn, resume) before giving access.
